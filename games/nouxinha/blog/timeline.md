# Nouxinha, version by version

Twenty-seven points in the game's history, from the first commit on 17 August 2026 to today. Each
one is that commit checked out and played the same way: the title screen, then a fixed walk of about
fifty steps out of the hut (down, left, down, right…). From the slot era onwards (version 06),
there is also a run with cheats on, which shows the late game, and its map.

Every version opens on the same world: `DEFAULT_SEED`, "noux". The pictures show one place being
redrawn over seven weeks. From version 16, when biomes arrive, that seed turns out to be a
mystical realm, which is why the timeline turns from green to magenta.
