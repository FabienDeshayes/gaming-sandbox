The sets

What every piece does, set by set. Every run plays the core set plus special sets drawn from its seed. Edit a piece's numbers and rule here and try it on the board at the right, which scores with the game's own rules; export writes src/data/pieces.js. A piece's name and the words on its card are src/text.js (WORDS), its pixels are DRAW. Unsaved edits are kept in this browser.
